[Home](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/) 2. Our services # Our services Last Updated Thursday, 05/02/2026 We deliver a range of consular services for Australians travelling or living overseas. We deliver our services through Australian embassies, high commissions and consulates overseas. **Understand when and how we can help. Read the**[**Consular Services Charter**](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/consular-services-charter "Consular Services Charter")**.** * [Travel advisories](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/consular-services#traveladvice) * [Subscription service](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/consular-services#subscribe) * [General travel advice](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/consular-services#general) * [Legalising documents](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/notarial-services "Notarial services") * [Overseas voting](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/consular-services#voting) * [Passport services](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/consular-services#passports) * [Consular emergency support](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/consular-services#cec) * [Crisis response](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/consular-services#cec) * [Information in other languages](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/consular-services#communities) * [Resources](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/consular-services#resources) ## Travel advice for destinations We prepare and publish [travel advice](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/what-our-travel-advice-levels-mean "What our travel advice levels mean") for over 170 destinations. We assign an [advice level](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/what-our-travel-advice-levels-mean "What our travel advice levels mean") to each destination. * [Travel advice explained](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/node/33) * Travel advice for your[destinations](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations) ## Subscription service We offer a subscription service. You can subscribe to stay in the loop when we update your destination's travel advisory. * [Subscribe to updates](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/smartraveller-subscription "Smartraveller subscription") for your destinations. ## General advice on travel topics We provide general advice on a range of travel topics. See our advice for * [Before you go](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/before-you-go "Before you go") * [While you're away](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/while-youre-away "While you’re away") ## Voting overseas We offer some voting services overseas. We deliver these services at some of our embassies, high commissions and consulates. * [Voting while overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/voting-overseas "Voting overseas") ## Legalising documents (notarial services) We offer a range of [notarial services](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/notarial-services "Notarial services"). * [Documents in Australia](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/notarial-services/how-get-your-documents-legalised "How to get your documents legalised") * [Documents overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/notarial-services/how-request-notarial-services-overseas "How to request notarial services overseas") ## Passport services We offer passport services in Australia through the [Australian Passports Office](https://www.passports.gov.au/). We deliver passport services overseas through our embassies, high commissions and consulates. * [Passport services](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/passport-services "Passport services") ## Consular emergency support and crisis response We provide urgent support for Australians overseas. We deliver this service though our [embassies, high commissions and consulates.](https://dfat.gov.au/about-us/our-locations/missions/pages/our-embassies-and-consulates-overseas.aspx) We also support Australians through our 24-hour [Consular Emergency Centre](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/i-need-urgent-help "I need urgent help") in Canberra. In some situations, we initiate a crisis response. This is usually when a [crisis](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/while-overseas/what-do-crisis "What to do in a crisis")affects many Australians in a destination. * [Consular emergency support](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/node/137) * Our [crisis response](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/while-overseas/what-do-crisis "What to do in a crisis") ## Information in other languages We provide the Consular Services Charter and general travel information in 6 other languages. * [Communities](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/communities "Communities") ## Resources We offer printable brochures on different travel topics, and offer bulk printing to businesses. * [Resources](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/resources "Resources") ## See also * Learn about the [services](https://dfat.gov.au/about-us/our-services/Pages/our-services.aspx)delivered by the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT). * Find contact details for Australian [embassies, high commissions and consulates](https://dfat.gov.au/about-us/our-locations/missions/pages/our-embassies-and-consulates-overseas.aspx) (DFAT). * Getting or renewing a [passport](https://www.passports.gov.au/)(Australian Passport Office). ## Related content [How to get a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/notarial-services/how-get-certificate-no-impediment-marriage) If you're going overseas to get married, you may need a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI) to prove you're free to marry.
