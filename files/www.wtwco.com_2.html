Increases in social unrest have threatened the rule of law, while civil war, active assailants and cyber-initiated damage have broadened the scope of concern. As we adjust to these shifts, extremists continue to operate, strategically leveraging hostility as a political tool. Now more than ever, risk managers should consider how to manage their exposure both domestically and on the global scale.
